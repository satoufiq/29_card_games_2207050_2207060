How to Play 29

The deck & the teams

32 cards (7 to Ace in four suits). Four players in two partnerships sit opposite each other: Team A (you & North) and Team B (West & East). Dealing, bidding and play always go clockwise.

Rank in every suit: J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7. Points: J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1 — 28 card points in the deck (29 with the optional last-trick point).

Bidding

Everyone gets 4 cards. Starting with the player after the dealer and going clockwise, bidding is a series of duels. The first player opens (16 or more); the next player must bid higher or pass. The player holding the bid may hold at the same number — they never need to go higher. When one of the two passes, the survivor duels the next player. Passing is final; the last player standing wins the contract.

Trump

The winner secretly picks a trump suit — or lets their concealed 7th card decide. While everyone still holds only 4 cards, the defenders may double (×2) and the declaring side may then redouble (×4). After that the last four cards are dealt. Trump stays hidden until a player who cannot follow suit calls for the trump.

Play

Follow suit if you can. If you can't, you may discard or call for the trump and cut. The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The bidding team needs card points ≥ the contract.

Marriage & Single Hand

Holding the K + Q of trump is a royal marriage: it lowers your team's target by 4 if you declared, or raises the declarers' target by 4 if you defend. A Single Hand is a bold attempt to win alone — its exact conditions are set in the house rules.

Scoring

Make the contract: +1 match point (×2 doubled, ×4 redoubled). Fail: −1. First team to reach the match target wins; falling to minus the target loses.
